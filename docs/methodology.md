# Methodology

## 1. Data generation
A fixed random seed creates 1,800 synthetic tickets across channels, priorities, issue types, and support teams.

## 2. Validation
The cleaning stage checks required columns, parses timestamps, removes duplicate ticket IDs, validates numeric measures, and derives SLA attainment.

## 3. KPI framework
The analysis measures volume, SLA attainment, first-response time, resolution time, CSAT, escalation rate, and reopen rate.

## 4. Segmentation
KPIs are compared by channel, priority, issue type, support team, and month to identify operational concentration and trends.

## 5. Visualization
Charts focus on ticket demand, SLA performance, CSAT, and resolution time so operational findings can be communicated quickly.

## Limitations
Synthetic data is useful for demonstrating analytical technique but does not represent real support operations. Results should not be interpreted as actual customer or business performance.
