# Findings Framework

The project is designed to turn KPI output into operational decisions.

Review the generated reports in this order:

1. **Demand:** identify channels, issue types, and months with the highest ticket volume.
2. **SLA:** identify priority groups with the largest attainment gaps.
3. **Speed:** compare first-response and resolution times across teams and channels.
4. **Customer experience:** compare CSAT against response and resolution performance.
5. **Escalations:** identify issue types or priorities generating disproportionate escalation.
6. **Reopens:** investigate areas where cases may be resolved without fully addressing the customer need.

Because the source data is synthetic, conclusions should be treated as examples of analytical reasoning rather than real operational findings.
