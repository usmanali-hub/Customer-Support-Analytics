# Executive Summary

## Objective
Provide a compact framework for evaluating customer-support workload, service-level performance, and customer experience.

## Decision lens
A strong support operation is not defined by ticket volume alone. The useful combination is demand, response speed, resolution speed, SLA attainment, customer satisfaction, escalations, and reopenings.

## Recommended review sequence
1. Quantify demand and workload concentration.
2. Identify SLA gaps by priority.
3. Locate slow resolution areas.
4. Compare CSAT across channels and issue types.
5. Investigate escalation and reopen patterns.
6. Translate recurring patterns into process, staffing, or knowledge-base improvements.

## Data caveat
All records are synthetic and are included solely to demonstrate a reproducible analytics workflow.
